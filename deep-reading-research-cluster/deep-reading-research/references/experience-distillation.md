# Experience Distillation

This module answers: how do we turn logs into reusable method?

## What to keep

- problem clusters
- failure symptoms
- final successful fix
- verification
- confidence

## What to drop

- noisy chronological attempts
- redundant self-correction loops
- incidental environment chatter

## Distillation Rule

Do not copy raw logs into the skill.
Extract the behavior rule that survived the debugging session.

## Problem Clustering

Cluster by:

- target relevance
- root cause
- repeated symptom
- repair strategy

## Experience Data

Use experience data when the conclusion may still be overturned later.
This keeps the workflow honest without pretending the evidence is final.
